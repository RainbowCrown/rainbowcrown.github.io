Undergraduate Thesis: Event-Triggered Predefined-Time Adaptive Control of a Location-Aware Rigid-Flexible Coupled Robot

  • Developed a rigid-flexible surgical robot prototype combining a Franka Emika Panda, a two-DoF continuum segment, Hall-IMU sensing, and a laser emitter.
  • Designed an event-triggered RBF neural network for online continuum-Jacobian estimation and a constrained QP incorporating RCM and joint-limit constraints, with a predefined-time Dini-RNN real-time solver.
  • Integrated localization, adaptive control, and safety optimization; evaluated calibration, tracking, constraint satisfaction, disturbance rejection, and computation time in simulation and hardware.
  • Achieved 0.02 mm tip-localization MAE after calibration, with tracking and RCM errors below 2 mm; nominated for Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis (Top 5%).

Comparative Study of Model-Free Kinematic Control Strategies

  • Compared RBFNN, ZND, and GND for online Jacobian estimation under unknown manipulator kinematics.
  • Implemented controllers in MATLAB/CoppeliaSim and on a seven-DoF Franka Panda using C++/ROS at 1 kHz.
