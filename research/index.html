Research

Intelligent control, grounded in physical systems.

My work combines adaptive learning, constrained optimization, magnetic sensing, and experimental robotics to make complex manipulators more accurate and safer.

Undergraduate thesis

Location-aware rigid-flexible coupled robot

Event-triggered predefined-time adaptive control for a surgical robot under model uncertainty and safety constraints.

System

Integrated surgical robotics prototype

A rigid-flexible platform combining a Franka Emika Panda manipulator, a two-DoF continuum segment, Hall-IMU sensing, and a laser emitter for simulated minimally invasive ablation.

Method

Adaptive estimation and safe optimization

An event-triggered RBF neural network estimates the continuum Jacobian online. A constrained quadratic program enforces remote-center-of-motion and joint-limit constraints, solved in real time by a predefined-time, noise-tolerant Dini-RNN.

Result

Sub-millimeter localization

After calibration, continuum-tip localization achieved a 0.02 mm mean absolute error and 0.45 mm maximum error. Physical experiments maintained both trajectory-tracking and RCM errors below 2 mm. The work was nominated for Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis at Sun Yat-sen University (Top 5%).

Comparative study

Model-free kinematic control

A controlled comparison of RBFNN, zeroing neural dynamics, and gradient neural dynamics for online Jacobian estimation and trajectory tracking.

Theory

Convergence analysis

Finite-time Lyapunov arguments, adaptive RBF weight updates, and error-coupling terms improve convergence under kinematic uncertainty.

Simulation

Matched conditions

Controllers were evaluated in MATLAB and CoppeliaSim for tracking accuracy, convergence, sensing-noise robustness, and computation time.

Hardware

1 kHz deployment

All three controllers were implemented in C++/ROS on a seven-DoF Franka Emika Panda at a 1 kHz control frequency.

Independent study · 2025

Simulation, graphics, and manipulation

Self-directed projects extended my work from robot control into physics-based simulation and learned manipulation.

MAPLE · ICRA 2022

Independent pipeline reproduction

Implemented eight parameterized behavior primitives for six-DoF manipulation and evaluated the reproduced MAPLE system against a SAC baseline on object-assembly tasks.

Mar–Sep 2025

Physics-based simulation and graphics

Studied numerical integration, physical simulation, shader programming, and dynamics using NUS HCRL materials, DreamWorks Animation resources, and graphics-course exercises. Reproduced small-scale cloth-simulation experiments to examine numerical stability, plausibility, and visual realism.
