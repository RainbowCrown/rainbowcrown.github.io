Welcome · Site introduction

Why I created this space

Beyond the finished papers and polished project summaries, research is full of small decisions worth documenting.

This website began as a place to present my work in robotics and artificial intelligence. The blog gives it another purpose: capturing the process behind that work.

A CV is good at recording outcomes - a prototype, a paper, a patent, an award. It is less suited to the practical details that make those outcomes possible: why an experiment failed, how an implementation changed, or what a paper made me reconsider.

What I plan to write about

The main themes will follow my research interests: model-free kinematic control, adaptive neural methods, continuum-robot sensing, constrained optimization, and physics-based simulation. I also hope to share notes on research tools and the transition from mathematical models to physical experiments.

This first article is intentionally a starting point. Future posts can be written as independent HTML pages and added to the blog index.

A record of learning

I see this space less as a stream of announcements and more as a durable research notebook. Clear writing exposes gaps in understanding, and public notes can make technical work easier for others to reproduce and discuss.

If any of these topics overlap with your work, feel free to get in touch.
