Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Researcher

Sep 2024 – Present

Working on model-free control, continuum-robot localization, adaptive estimation, and safe real-time optimization for robotic manipulators.

Rigid-flexible surgical robot

Developed a prototype combining a Franka Emika Panda, a two-DoF continuum segment, Hall-IMU sensing, and a laser emitter. The system integrates magnetic localization, an event-triggered RBF neural estimator, and RCM-constrained control.

Result: 0.02 mm continuum-tip localization MAE after calibration, with physical tracking and RCM errors below 2 mm.

Model-free kinematic control

Compared RBF neural networks, zeroing neural dynamics, and gradient neural dynamics under consistent simulation and hardware conditions. All three controllers were deployed on a seven-DoF Franka Panda using C++/ROS at 1 kHz.

Independent technical studies

Reproduced the core MAPLE manipulation pipeline and studied physics-based simulation, numerical integration, shader programming, and cloth simulation through independent projects.
