About

Robots that understand their constraints.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University in 2026, with a GPA of 3.9/4.0. At the COIR Lab, advised by Prof. Weibing Li, my work has focused on model-free robot control, continuum-robot shape sensing, and safe optimization for minimally invasive surgical robotics.
